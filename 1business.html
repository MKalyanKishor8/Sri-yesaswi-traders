Commodity Catalog

Our 5 Core Commodities

Directly aggregated, laboratory tested for purity, and packaged to meet export and domestic bulk specifications.

Pulses & Legumes

1. Premium Rajma Seeds

Red Kidney Beans, Chitra & Sharmili varieties. 100% Sortex machine cleaned, uniform bold grain size, rich in protein. Moisture < 11%, Purity 99.5%.

Spices & Roots

2. Finger Turmeric

High Curcumin (3.5% – 5.2%), Double Polished, Nizamabad & Salem Export Grades. 100% natural whole fingers, moisture < 10%, zero artificial coloring.

Whole Spices

3. Premium Black Pepper

Bold 500GL – 570GL density, sun-dried, high piperine content (5% – 7%). Garbled & Ungarbled, pinhead cleaned, moisture < 12%, rich pungent aroma.

Plantation Raw Seeds

4. Raw Green Coffee Seeds

Unroasted Arabica Plantation-A, Robusta Cherry & Parchment green beans. Screen 17/18, uniform moisture (10% – 12%), ideal for commercial roasters.

Spices & Roots

5. Dried Whole & Powder Ginger

Sun-dried, bleached/unbleached Cochin & Calicut grades. High gingerol pungency, bold fiber-free slices (Sonth), moisture < 12%.
